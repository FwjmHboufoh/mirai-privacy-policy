Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Introduction

Mirai Kuriyama is a Discord bot that provides AI chatbot, Growtopia utilities, moderation tools, and entertainment features. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when using the bot.

Information We Process

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Message content used to process commands and AI interactions
  • Information necessary to provide requested features

How We Use Information

  • Provide AI chatbot responses
  • Process user commands
  • Provide Growtopia-related utilities and statistics
  • Support moderation and community features
  • Improve service reliability and performance

Message Content Intent

Mirai Kuriyama uses Discord's Message Content Intent to detect chatbot triggers, process text-based commands, respond to mentions, and generate contextual AI responses requested by users.

Data Sharing

Mirai Kuriyama does not sell, rent, or trade user data. Information is only processed as necessary to provide bot functionality.

Data Retention

Data may be temporarily stored for functionality, security, debugging, and service operation purposes. Data is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the bot.

Third-Party Services

The bot may utilize third-party services and APIs required to provide certain features. These services may process information necessary to fulfill user requests.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the bot owner through the official support server.
